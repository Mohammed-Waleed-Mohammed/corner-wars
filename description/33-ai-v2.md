# 33 — AI v2: a Commander that thinks (economy, intel, priorities, defense, war)

File 22 built the Commander's skeleton: three layers on three clocks, personalities, fog of war,
FightOdds, the ring-wall base planner. This file replaces the parts of it that decide **what to do
with gold, what to believe about the enemy, what matters most right now, how to hold the base, and
when and where to fight**. File 22 still holds for everything this file does not restate (cadences,
determinism, perception, formations, the base planner, Citadel powers, the F5 overlay).

Hard rules (unchanged from 22): the AI acts only through commands; its only randomness is the seeded
`cs.rngState`; it decides only from its own perception (22 §C v3); Easy keeps the old brain.

## §0 Why: what the old Commander actually did (measured, `npm run aisim -- survey`)

Measured with the new headless harness (§T) before any change:

| what | measured |
|---|---|
| income after the home mine runs out (min 6–24, 4-player FFA) | **≈ 4 g/s** for every AI, all match |
| refineries per AI, whole match | 1–2, never an expansion |
| Resonance Taps built | **0** (stuck "saving for resonanceTapping" at 4 g/s) |
| workers | 8–10, never more |
| stance | PRESSURE ≈ 90 % of the time — the Turtle included |
| duels | decided by the first big fight at 5–9 min |
| 4-player FFA | no winner after 24 min; armies of 60–77 units bleed out, nothing replaces them |

Measured economy rules the new design is built on (`npm run aisim -- exp`):

| workers on one mine | with a Refinery beside it | delivering to a yard 15 tiles away |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 10.8 g/s | 1.8 g/s |
| 6 | 21.8 g/s | 5.0 g/s |
| 10 | 29.2 g/s | 7.8 g/s |
| 16 | 35.1 g/s | 12.2 g/s |

* A Refinery at the mine is a **4–6× multiplier**. Every mine worth mining gets one.
* A mine lasts **2–5 minutes** at full speed (home 5000, mid 3000, rich 8000). Mines run out by
  minute 10–12; after that, only Resonance Taps (4–6 g/s each, forever) and the Citadel (2 g/s) pay.
* Every map is ×3 scaled: mines are 15–45 tiles from the yard, and a building must stand within
  BUILD_RADIUS = 6 of one you own. **An expansion needs a chain of stepping-stone buildings.** The old
  expansion code never built one; that is why it never expanded.

## §B Intel — what the AI believes, and how it finds out

### §B.1 Beliefs (`cs.beliefs`, rebuilt every operational tick from the perception view)

Per living enemy `e`:

| field | how |
|---|---|
| `known` | ArmyValue of e's fighters in sight + remembered (22 §C v3 memory) |
| `est` | `known + unseen growth`: e may have built since the AI last had a good look at its army: `+ econEst(e) × MIL_SHARE × seconds since lastGoodLook`, capped at `UNSEEN_GROWTH_CAP` |
| `lastGoodLook` | last tick ≥ `GOOD_LOOK_FRAC` of `known` was in sight at once |
| `armyAt` | centroid of e's known fighters (where the army is believed to be) |
| `home` | e's main yard (known from the map) |
| `away` | the army centroid is ≥ `EXPOSED_DIST` from e's yard (the base is exposed) |
| `econEst` | g/s: seen workers × rate (with a refinery near them) + taps × 4 + Citadel; unseen → `ECON_GUESS` |
| `comp` | gold-weighted class mix of known units (feeds the counter mix) |
| `tech` | inferred: seen Heavy Tank / Artillery / AT Cannon / Missile Tower / Tap |
| `baseDef` | DefenseScore at e's main yard (known towers) |
| `expansions` | e's known refineries / taps / second yard away from its main |
| `attackedMeTick` | last tick e's fighters were inside my territory (grudge) |
| `incoming` | ArmyValue of e's fighters in sight, within `ALERT_RADIUS` of my base and closing (distance to my yard fell ≥ `CLOSING_TILES` since the last tick) or already within `HOME_ALERT_RADIUS` |

Map:

| field | how |
|---|---|
| `mines[]` | every gold source: last-known gold, `mine` (I have a drop-off ≤ CLAIM_RADIUS), `theirs` (an enemy building ≤ CLAIM_RADIUS), `danger` (enemy fighters seen ≤ SOURCE_THREAT_RADIUS within the last `DANGER_MEMORY_TICKS`), walking-ish distance from my yard |
| `alert` | the strongest `incoming` (value, from where, ETA at the attacker's speed) |

### §B.2 Information collection

1. **Scout (all tiers).** One fast unit (Scout Buggy; a stand-in fighter until one exists) tours the
   point of interest with the highest `importance × staleness`: an enemy main (importance 3 while the
   strategic layer is considering an attack or that enemy's `est` is mostly guess, else 2), known enemy
   expansions (2), unclaimed mines (1, that is where expansions appear), the Citadel (1). It looks from a
   **peek point** — outside the known towers' reach, `PEEK_DIST` short of the target on its own side —
   and runs from any real fight. A push objective unseen for `ATTACK_INTEL_TICKS` is scouted first
   (22 §K v3, kept).
2. **Sentries (Hard).** One cheap unit holds a watch post on each approach to my base: on the line to
   each of the 2 nearest enemies, `SENTRY_OUT` tiles beyond my wall. Its sight feeds `incoming` (an
   early warning 20–30 tiles before the army reaches the wall). It falls back from anything that
   would kill it and returns `SENTRY_RETURN_TICKS` later.
3. **Everything else that sees counts:** perception already remembers whatever any unit or
   building of mine saw. Beliefs are only as good as what the AI has seen, so the AI never cheats.

## §C Economy

Rules, in the order the economy planner evaluates them each operational tick. Each produces a
**request** for the arbiter (§D); nothing spends directly.

1. **Workers.** Target = `WORKERS_PER_MINE × active mines + BUILDER_SPARE`, capped at `WORKER_CAP` and
   `WORKER_CAP_FRAC × unit cap`. An *active mine* is one of mine with gold and a drop-off within
   CLAIM_RADIUS, or one with a Refinery being built, or the mine of a running expansion project. The
   yard queues workers continuously below target (`WORKER_QUEUE` deep). The War Factory helps when
   the deficit is large.
2. **Refinery at every active mine.** A mine I mine with no Refinery beside it gets one at once
   (priority 95). If no legal spot near the mine is within build radius yet, that is an expansion
   project (rule 4) even for the home mine.
3. **Power.** Projected surplus (finished + sites under construction + requests about to be placed)
   must stay ≥ POWER_SURPLUS_MIN. A plant is requested *before* the consumer that would break it.
4. **Expansion projects.** Start the next one when (a) my active mines will run dry within
   `EXPAND_LEAD_S` at the current rate, or (b) I have more workers than my active mines can use, or
   (c) the opening timer `EXPAND_FIRST_S` has passed and a safe mine is free. Pick the mine with the
   best `gold / (1 + dist / EXPAND_DIST_NORM)` that is not `theirs`, not in `danger`, and nearer to me
   than to any enemy base (contested central mines need an army lead). A project is a chain:
   * **creep:** while no legal Refinery spot near the mine is within build radius, place a stepping
     stone, the legal footprint within build radius closest to the target. Outside my wall the stone
     is a Pillbox (vision and defense on the route); inside, a Power Plant when power is needed.
     At most `CREEP_MAX_STEPS`.
   * **Refinery** beside the mine, then the **guard tower** (Pillbox, or a Gun Turret for defense-
     budget ≥ 0.12 personalities).
   * Workers move in by rule 6. The project is abandoned when the mine turns `theirs`/`danger` or a
     step can't be placed for `PROJECT_STALL_TICKS`.
   One project at a time; two at once once income ≥ `EXPAND_PARALLEL_INCOME`.
5. **Mining research and the Resonance Grid.** Improved Mining I as soon as the Lab stands (payback
   about a minute at 30 g/s), then Mining II. Resonance Tapping as soon as income is stable
   (`TAP_START_S`, or earlier when projected mine gold left ÷ income < `TAP_MINES_LEFT_S`). Taps up to
   4 as gold allows, each preceded by its Power Plant; Deep Resonance once 3 Taps stand. The priority
   rises as the mines run out (§D table), so the Grid is in place before the gold is gone.
6. **Worker upkeep (kept from 22 §F v2, extended).** Idle workers go to the active mine with the most
   room; over-saturated mines shed to under-saturated ones; a depleted mine's crew moves to the next
   active mine; with no active mine left, workers long-distance mine the safest mine with gold
   (better than idle) — but never one deep on a rival's side, such as their natural, where a crew
   with no Refinery walks past their army unguarded; with none, they wait at home (39 §E). **Evacuation:** a mine with enemy fighters at it that my fighters and towers
   there can't beat loses its crew to the safest other mine (or the yard) until it is clear.
7. **Bank discipline.** Gold above `FLOAT_LIMIT` with nothing to save for adds producers (§F.4).

## §D Priorities — the budget arbiter

Every subsystem posts requests `{ key, cat, cost, prio, act }`. The arbiter sorts them by priority
(highest first; ties by key for determinism) and walks the list with the real gold:

* affordable → run it, subtract its cost;
* not affordable and `prio ≥ SAVE_PRIO` → **save for it**: every request below it may spend only the
  gold above that cost (`SAVE_LEAK` lets cheap essentials, workers and power, through);
* construction requests also need a **site slot**: `SITES_BASE + workers / SITES_PER_WORKERS`, with
  economy and survival items allowed `SITE_SLACK` more.

Danger `D ∈ [0, 1]` (§E.1) shifts the table: survival rises, investments fall.

| request | priority |
|---|---|
| refinery at an active mine | 95 |
| power plant needed now / for the next placement | 92 |
| worker below target | 90 − 25·D |
| emergency tower (§E.4) | 88 |
| army unit while army < desired, or D > 0 | 70 + 25·D |
| first Barracks | 87 |
| expansion step (creep, refinery, guard) | 70, or 89 when mines run dry within EXPAND_LEAD_S |
| Improved Mining I / II | 74 / 62 |
| Lab | 66 |
| War Factory | 64 |
| Resonance Tapping / Tap | 55 / 52, rising to 80 / 78 when mines left < TAP_MINES_LEFT_S |
| Supply Lines (at ≥ 85 % cap) | 80 |
| core defenses (plan) | 50 + 30·D |
| extra producer (spend capacity < income share) | 60 |
| army unit while army ≥ desired | 45 |
| Weapons / Armor | 40 + army value / 300 (capped 70) |
| Deep Resonance | 58 |
| walls (plan) | 35 (Turtle 55) |
| other research | 30 |

In DEFEND every investment item (Lab, research, Taps, expansion, walls) is capped at 40 so
survival takes all the gold. `cs.saveFor` (32 §D) becomes the arbiter's saving line; the F5 overlay shows it.

## §E Defense mechanisms

1. **Danger.** `D = max(Dnow, Dincoming, Dstrategic)`:
   * `Dnow`: the territory threat (22 §C v2) against what holds it (FightOdds there, my units and
     towers): `clamp(1.2 − odds, 0, 1)` when the threat is ≥ DEFEND_MIN_VALUE.
   * `Dincoming`: the `alert` value against my army + towers at the gate.
   * `Dstrategic`: the strongest enemy `est` against `desiredArmy` (below), half weight.
2. **Desired army.** `max(MIN_ARMY(t), threatEst × personality.safety)`, where threatEst = the
   strongest enemy `est` (+ `FFA_SECOND_W` × the second strongest), minus `DEF_ARMY_CREDIT` × my
   tower value. Below it, army production outranks investment; above it, investment wins.
3. **Response ladder** (tactical, at the threat point):
   * home guard + towers hold it (FightOdds ≥ BASE_HOLD_ODDS): the main army keeps its job;
   * otherwise the main army comes home (22 §K v4, kept);
   * **fight under towers:** if my force wins only with my towers' help, it holds at the *defense
     anchor* (the strongest tower group between the threat and the yard) instead of walking into the
     open; the attacker has to come to it;
   * **workers:** evacuation (§C.6).
4. **Static defense.** Three sources of tower requests:
   * the base plan's towers (22 §G v4, kept): core first, extras by defense budget;
   * **adaptive**: every place my territory was attacked is remembered (`cs.hits`, decaying); the most-hit
     approach gets a counter tower (Pillbox vs infantry, AT Cannon vs heavy when unlocked, Missile
     Tower vs big groups, Gun Turret otherwise), up to `ADAPTIVE_MAX` per approach;
   * **emergency**: an `alert` that my army + towers at the gate can't hold, arriving later than a
     tower's build time → one counter tower at the gate post (at most one per `EMERGENCY_GAP_TICKS`).
5. **Expansions** get a guard tower (§C.4); the home guard answers raids it can win (22 §J v2).
6. **Repair** (22 §F v2) and **rebuild**: a lost Refinery, Barracks, War Factory or Power Plant shows up
   as a missing request again (the planners count what exists), so it is rebuilt by priority.

## §F War strategy

1. **Posture** (strategic tick; first match wins; Medium runs rows 1–2 + personality default):
   1. DEFEND: `Dnow ≥ DEFEND_DANGER`, or `Dincoming ≥ DEFEND_DANGER` with ETA < `ALERT_ETA_S` (hold
      until Dnow < DEFEND_EXIT_DANGER).
   2. RECOVER: lost > RECOVER_LOSS_FRAC of my army in RECOVER_WINDOW and below desired. Rebuild
      behind my towers.
   3. ALL_IN (window, 45 s): the target is crippled (low power, just lost ALLIN_ENEMY_DROP of its army),
      **or its army is away** and mine reaches its base first with projected odds ≥ 1, **or I'm at the
      unit cap** with odds ≥ 1 (units I can't add are worth spending).
   4. PRESSURE: my army ≥ attackRatio × the target's `est` (belief, not a peek), or the personality's
      first-push value is reached and I'm not behind.
   5. BUILD_UP.
2. **Target (FFA)**: 22 §E scoring, read from beliefs (`est` instead of what is in sight), plus
   `W.EXPOSED` for an enemy whose army is `away`, and never two wars at once.
3. **Objective.** Within the target: its army in the field if I can beat it where it stands, else the
   best building by the 22 §K v2 victim score: value × damage ÷ tower cover ÷ distance. That score
   already prefers soft expansions to fortified mains, so the AI **punishes expansions** first.
4. **Spend capacity.** Producers: `ceil(income × MIL_SHARE / PRODUCER_RATE)`, clamped
   `[PRODUCERS_MIN, PRODUCERS_MAX]`. Queues are kept `QUEUE_DEPTH` deep, not 4–12, so gold isn't
   locked in queues the AI might need elsewhere.
5. **Harass (Hard).** Every personality but the Turtle runs one raid squad of Scout Buggies once its
   army ≥ `RAID_MIN_ARMY` (the Opportunist two). Raids go for the enemy mine with the least tower
   cover and flee fair fights (22 §J v2).
6. **Push discipline (kept from 22 §K):** look before you leap, gather out of tower reach, engage as
   one body, raze loose, retreat at the contact point, regroup before re-pushing, finishing blow.

## §T The scenario suite (`npm run aisim -- scen [filter]`)

A headless harness (`src/dev/aisim/`, never in the game bundle) builds matches the way the game does
and steps them like the LocalSession. A scenario stages a situation (an idle or scripted opponent,
spawned forces, a damaged economy), runs it, and checks an outcome. **Every scenario must pass**
before the AI ships, plus `det` (two identical runs, identical checksums).

### §T.1 What the suite caught (and the fixes)

Writing the scenarios before tuning found real faults, each fixed in the AI rather than the test:

- **First mine** — with no income the expansion picker still scored gold/distance and walked to the far rich mine; with no active mine it now takes the nearest viable one (eco.opening).
- **Early warning** — the alert radius was a flat 45 tiles and a sighting was lost the moment the sentry ran; the radius now scales with the distance between bases, an army on my half counts on first sight, the alert outlives its sighting (march extrapolated, ALERT_MEMORY_TICKS), and sentries stand 22 tiles beyond the wall (intel.warning).
- **Investment under attack** — armory research was filed as military and slipped through DEFEND; all research is an investment now, and investments are dropped (not just capped) while DEFEND holds or danger ≥ DEFEND_DANGER (prio.threat).
- **Expansions** — the guard tower waited for the Refinery to finish, at low priority; it is now raised alongside it (def.expansion).
- **Research chains** — a prerequisite kept the priority of its own entry, so the Grid's urgency never reached Mining II and a Techer finished an FFA with zero Taps; a chain step now inherits its most urgent goal.
- **Retreats** — odds were read from the vanguard alone, so a strung-out column always looked beaten and routed; a committed push now counts members about to arrive (ARRIVE_TILES) and retreats only on a losing read that holds RETREAT_CONFIRM_TICKS.
- **Breaching** — a formation's anchor cannot path into a walled base, so pushes stalled for minutes outside it; a push that stops closing on its objective for PUSH_STALL_TICKS breaks formation and attack-moves loose (Hard vs Medium went from 1/3 to 3/3).
- **The win condition** — the yard is the only thing that eliminates (22 §W1); its victim weight is now 12, not 3.
- **Fortification belief** — counted towers within 10 tiles of the yard, but the ring stands 12–16 out on the scaled maps, so no base ever looked fortified and siege never came; it reads BASE_DEF_RADIUS 18 now, and Siege Doctrine is wanted from defense score 25.
- **The Citadel** — a squad lost there was replaced every 60 s; each loss in a row now doubles the wait.

Tried and reverted: shrinking the home guard near the unit cap won FFA pushes but lost the Medium duels to counter-attacks, so the guard keeps the personality's share.

Results at commit: every scenario passes; war.vsMedium 3/3; war.ffa 2/3 (winners at ~38 and ~40 min, the third down to 2 players at 50 min).
